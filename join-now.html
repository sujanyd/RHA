How You Can Contribute

Choose the way that suits you best. Every role is crucial to our mission.

1

Food Distribution

Be on the ground distributing meals to people in need. This is the heart of what we do.

  • Weekly or monthly drives
  • Build direct connections
  • Flexible timing
  • Team activities
2

Food Collection

Help collect surplus food from restaurants, stores, and communities before it goes to waste.

  • Coordinate with restaurants
  • Quality food sourcing
  • Transportation support
  • Early morning or evening
3

Event Coordination

Organize and lead community events, fundraisers, and awareness campaigns.

  • Plan large-scale events
  • Community engagement
  • Leadership opportunities
  • Strategic planning
4

Online Support

Contribute from home through social media, graphic design, content, or administrative work.

  • Work remotely
  • Creative roles
  • Social media management
  • Data & documentation

Getting Started is Simple

1

Find Your Chapter

Search for Robin Hood Army in your city. We operate in 406+ cities worldwide. Connect with your local chapter.

2

Attend an Event

Join us for a distribution drive or orientation. No prior experience needed. Come as you are.

3

Start Contributing

Volunteer regularly, check in your activities, and become part of our community. Scale up your involvement as you grow.

4

Grow as a Leader

Move into leadership roles, mentoring others, and shaping the future of your chapter and the movement.
