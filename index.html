The Silent Crisis

Millions of people go to bed hungry every single night, while massive amounts of food go to waste. We are the bridge between surplus and survival.

Over 190 million people in India are undernourished, facing the daily struggle of extreme hunger.

Families are often forced to choose between medicine, education, and their next warm meal.

Nearly 40% of food produced is wasted, while millions suffer from chronic malnutrition.
