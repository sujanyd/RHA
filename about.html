About Robin Hood Army

A volunteer-driven movement dedicated to ending hunger and creating a more equitable world.

Our Mission

What We Do

We collect surplus food from restaurants, stores, and communities, and distribute it to people in need. It's about making food available consistently to everyone who needs it.

Why We Do It

Hunger is not a resource problem — it's a distribution problem. In a world of abundance, no one should sleep hungry. We believe in simple solutions to complex problems.

How We Do It

Through a network of passionate volunteers across 400+ cities, we've distributed over 160 million meals. Every Robin, every restaurant, every community member plays a crucial role.

Our Core Values

These principles guide everything we do at Robin Hood Army.

👥

Citizens First

Every decision we make is guided by what's best for the people we serve and the volunteers who power our mission.

🎯

Mission Next

The mission of ending hunger comes before everything else, including organizational interests or individual recognition.

💪

Robins Last

We celebrate our volunteers not for personal glory, but for their contribution to the greater cause and community impact.

Our Growth Journey

From a small group of friends to a global movement for change.

2014-2016: The Beginning

Started as a group of friends coming together on weekends to distribute surplus food. Expanded from Delhi to multiple Indian cities. Built the foundation of our volunteer network.

2M+ Meals Served

2017-2019: Scaling Up

Launched #Mission1Million, mobilized media and corporates. Featured on Harvard Business School as a case study. Expanded to 200+ cities and partnered with major organizations.

28M+ Meals Served

2020-Present: Global Impact

Launched #Mission30 during COVID, serving 23.2M meals across 8 countries. Started Robin Hood Academy for education. Operating in 406+ cities with 266K+ volunteers worldwide.

160M+ Meals Served
