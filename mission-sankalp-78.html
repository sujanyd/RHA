Mission Sankalp 78

A transformative national initiative to eliminate food insecurity across India and build sustainable, community-driven solutions for hunger.

What is Mission Sankalp 78?

A comprehensive nationwide campaign to reach 78 states, regions, and territories with sustainable food distribution and community empowerment programs.

🎯

Our Goal

Establish and strengthen Robin Hood Army chapters in 78 regions, creating a network of trained volunteers dedicated to fighting hunger and building food security at the grassroots level.

🤝

Community Partnership

Build lasting partnerships with local restaurants, NGOs, government bodies, and community organizations to create sustainable food distribution systems that don't rely on seasonal or temporary funding.

📊

Measurable Impact

Target to distribute 500 million meals over the next 3 years, train 500,000+ volunteers, and create permanent livelihood opportunities for 10,000+ individuals from underprivileged backgrounds.

Four Pillars of Mission Sankalp 78

Our comprehensive approach to sustainable hunger elimination.

01

Distribution

Systematic collection of surplus food from restaurants, hotels, catering services, and households. Daily distribution to vulnerable populations ensuring no edible food goes to waste.

1000+ collection points nationwide
02

Education

Robin Hood Academy providing vocational training, digital literacy, and life skills to children from low-income families. Preparing the next generation for self-sufficiency.

50,000+ students reached
03

Advocacy

Influencing policy changes, raising awareness about food waste, and mobilizing corporate and governmental support for sustainable food security systems.

50+ policy recommendations
04

Empowerment

Creating income-generating opportunities for volunteers through micro-entrepreneurship, skill development, and livelihood programs that support dignity and independence.

10,000+ livelihoods created
